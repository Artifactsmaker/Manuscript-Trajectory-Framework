## Overview

The **Manuscript Trajectory Framework (MTF)** is a conceptual and computational framework for analyzing, classifying, and positioning scientific manuscripts within a multidimensional publication ecosystem.

Unlike traditional publication systems that focus primarily on acceptance or rejection decisions, MTF models manuscripts as **evolving knowledge artifacts** moving through a publication space composed of whitepapers, preprints, conference papers, journal articles, and long-term research programs.

The framework introduces a trajectory-centered perspective on scientific publishing and provides mechanisms for:

* Manuscript DNA Analysis
* Manuscript Classification
* Publication Positioning
* Journal Genome Matching
* Research Evolution Planning
* Manuscript Maturity Estimation
* Publication Intelligence

---

## Core Concepts

### Manuscript DNA

MTF represents manuscripts through seven developmental dimensions:

D(M) = (N, F, V, R, U, M, E)

where:

* N = Novelty
* F = Formalization
* V = Validation
* R = Reproducibility
* U = Reusability
* M = Vision Magnitude
* E = Research Expansion Potential

---

### Publication Trajectory

A manuscript is modeled as an evolving entity moving through publication space.

Example trajectory:

Idea → Whitepaper → Preprint → Conference Paper → Journal Article

---

### Publication Intelligence

MTF introduces the concept of **Publication Intelligence**, defined as the ability to analyze, understand, predict, and optimize manuscript development within publication ecosystems.

## Vision

MTF proposes a trajectory-centered view of scientific publishing.

Rather than viewing publication as a sequence of acceptance decisions, the framework models scientific manuscripts as evolving knowledge systems moving through publication space.

The long-term objective is to contribute toward a broader science of publication, publication intelligence systems, and AI-assisted research planning platforms.
